# Public copy reframe: noticing, saving and turning ideas into plans

Branch `public-pages-copy-reframe`, on top of v2.94.1 (`c79ef6c`). **Not deployed, merged, pushed or published.** Files changed: `public-content.js`, `public-pages.js`, `test/public-coldstart.js`. No change to `server.js`, `plugin/plugin.json`, the MCP implementation, OAuth, API keys, scopes, provenance, the schema, the Muse pages' facts or anything behind sign-in.

## 1. The revised narrative, in page order

**Homepage (`/`).** Brand line unchanged (*Your interests. Your travels. Wherever you go.*). Opening: *Discriminantly turns the places and things you notice—and the new possibilities your AI discovers—into plans you can share, copy and build together.* Then: *Save what catches your eye just by telling the AI assistant you already use. Start with a blank slate or draw on what you've saved, add recommendations, and shape it all into a day-by-day itinerary.* "Durable personal record" no longer carries the pitch; it appears later, in the AI section, as the explanation of continuity.

- **What you save** (was "What you keep"): six kinds of record, each means one thing. Intro rewritten in customer verbs (save, mark, find, organize, refine; save things in conversation; what you have saved and experienced sharpens recommendations). The six definitions are unchanged in meaning.
- **Noticed over time. Useful when you need it.** (was "Kept over time. Then used."), eyebrow *Notice something now. Bring it into a plan when you need it.* Framing paragraph now names restaurants, shops, objects, links and experiences scattered across chats, screenshots, tabs and bookmarks, brought back, combined with new recommendations and shaped into a plan; the cold-start clarification ("One workflow, not a required order…nothing saved") stays. The Kyoto example is unchanged in substance: step titles say "Save a maker's object" and "The plan is saved, shared and refined"; the tiles still show the application's own "Kept 4 March / kept 5 March"; the conversation says "starting from what I've saved".
- **Save. Find. Plan. Share. Return.** (was "Recall. Refine. Plan. Return."): six lines, now in the brief's sequence: save while already talking to AI; find it again without remembering where it was filed; recommendations from what you ask for; bring saved and newly discovered places into one plan; share, copy or build it together; return with check-ins.
- **Plan together**: unchanged.
- **Use it with AI**: *Save, find, discover and plan with the assistant you prefer.* Paragraph leads with the brief's two sentences (choice and utility; the record stays in Discriminantly so compatible assistants can work with the same material when you authorize them), then the durable-record explanation with revoke and provenance.
- **Closing**: a new line in the existing final block, *Save what you notice by saying so. Plan from scratch or from what you've saved. Share a plan, copy one, or build it with friends.*, directly above the unchanged control statement (private or public per record; choose and revoke assistants; see what each changed). No new section: the homepage still has exactly seven sections.

**How it works.** Lede: *Eight ways Discriminantly helps you save, organize, discover and plan…* Step 1 *Save an object, maker, idea or interest as a Note*; step 2 *Save a place as a Travel Mark*; step 5 *…places you have already saved, or both*; step 8 *Discriminantly records every change…*. "Adding a place to a plan keeps it as a mark" and "Recommendations stay marked as recommendations until you keep them" retained (adoption). Evidence block "Kept, visited, owned, endorsed: each its own act" retained.

**Record pages** (`/notes`, `/travel-marks`, `/check-ins`, `/collections`, `/itineraries`, `/warrants`). Breadcrumb and card eyebrow "What you keep" → "What you save". Descriptions and ledes in the action ("A note saves an object…", "A plan, saved; never a record of going.", "A place worth remembering, saved with its name and where it is."). Travel Marks: "People save marks for…", "Saving is not going." The semantic sections keep their wording: "What keeping one asserts", "Adding a place to a plan keeps it as a travel mark", "stay recommendations until kept".

**Use with AI (`/ai`).** Starting out now opens with what a connected assistant helps you accomplish: *save a restaurant, shop, object, link or experience when you ask; find what you have saved; recommend new possibilities; build a plan from scratch, from saved material or both; and save the resulting itinerary in Discriminantly.* Then the no-record-needed paragraph and the durable-record/stance paragraph ("what you save, what you plan, and where each came from"). Splash lead "What you save becomes useful." Client statuses, chips, FAQ and platform facts unchanged.

**Platform pages** (`/ai/chatgpt`, `/ai/claude`, `/ai/muse`, `/ai/other`). "What you can ask": *Start from a request, use your record, save what matters.* Suggested requests: *Start from a request* (Naples, unchanged), *Use what you've saved* → "Turn my saved London places into a weekend plan and recommend a few additions.", *Save something* → "Keep Hatchards in London for me." (conversational, retained). Verification copy says "the travel marks you have saved". Connection facts, review status, last-tested facts and Muse content unchanged in substance (one word in the ChatGPT last-tested basis: "a saved place").

**Guides.** Index description in customer verbs. *Save and organize objects and interests* (slug `keep-objects-and-interests` unchanged; question "How can I save objects and places in one personal record?"); *Plan a trip from what you've saved* (slug `plan-from-what-you-keep` unchanged). Section headings "What keeping asserts, and does not", "Recommendations are not marks / stay recommendations", "Each person keeps their own" retained because each explains adoption into a record.

**About.** "What it is" unchanged (proposition + stance). "Why it exists" rewritten to the concrete problem: *You notice things all the time. Most of them get lost.* — a restaurant someone mentions, a shop, a product, a link, an experience, scattered across chats, screenshots, browser tabs, notes and map pins; save them naturally without another filing system; later they turn into recommendations and plans an authorized AI can draw on, shared, copied or built with friends. The generic-AI-memory paragraph and the control sentences stay. The identity statement remains where it was, in "Who makes it", after the problem and value.

**Machine-readable.** `llms.txt` opens with the homepage opening, then the durable-record sentence; heading "What it keeps" → "What it records". Agent guidance unchanged (its "kept / keeps / Keeping is the member's act" are the semantic rules agents need).

- **Fuller description:** Save restaurants, shops, products, links and experiences by talking to your AI. Start from scratch or use what you've saved to get recommendations and build day-by-day itineraries you can share, copy or create with friends.
- **Suggested prompts:**
  1. "Plan two days in Naples around classic cafés, pizza and tailoring."

## 7. Semantic boundaries

Unchanged and asserted by the tests: Notes, Travel Marks, Check-ins, Collections, Itineraries and Warrants keep their definitions and "does not prove" lists; a saved object is not owned, a saved place is not visited, a plan is not a trip; recommendations remain recommendations until kept; check-ins and warrants are never inferred; no page claims automatic capture, tagging or inference; an existing record is not required; Discriminantly is not presented as a travel planner only (objects and interests remain first-class on the homepage and record pages); no claim of support for every assistant; Perplexity is not mentioned; connector facts, Muse status, authentication and MCP behaviour untouched.
